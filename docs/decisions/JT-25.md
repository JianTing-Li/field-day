# JT-25 decisions (demo polish from Yadan's review, 2026-11-02)

### D-JT25-1 — "No option avoids …" instead of options that can't be approved
- **Date / owner:** 2026-11-02 · JT (from Yadan's review)
- **Decision:** When the review returns no option, or every option still fails a critical check, the next step
  says "No option avoids the rain" (or the heat, the poor air quality, …) and offers "Edit the brief", instead of
  showing options with "Use this plan". The "Check the weather again" link is hidden when the next step is already
  "Check the latest weather".
- **Status:** in use

### D-JT25-2 — Far-off events are saved and wait for the forecast
- **Date / owner:** 2026-11-02 · JT (decided with the human)
- **PRD section:** §3 "Unknown is never treated as pass"; "critical gaps block finalization"
- **Decision:** An event beyond the forecast range is created, planned and saved as before, and shows "Waiting on
  forecast" instead of "Needs checking" (`isWaitingOnForecast` in `src/contracts/checks.ts`, `waiting_on_forecast` on
  the event summary), only when the weather or air quality is the last thing left. The next step names the day the forecast comes out ("around Sat, Nov 7") and has no button,
  since checking earlier can't help. It still can't be locked in until the weather and air quality are known.
- **Rejected:** locking in with "weather unknown" marked. The PRD's core rule is that an unknown critical check blocks
  finalization; a locked-in plan with no forecast would be the one thing FieldDay promises not to produce.
- **Status:** in use
